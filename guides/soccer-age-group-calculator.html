What age group
is my child in?

Youth soccer age groups changed for the 2026-27 season. US Club Soccer, US Youth Soccer and AYSO now form age groups from August 1 to July 31 instead of by calendar year, so a fall birthday no longer moves a child up a group. Enter a date of birth and get the answer for this season and next, with the game format they play.

Questions parents ask

What does the U mean?

“Under.” U-10 means the players are under 10 for the season, by the cutoff rule. It says nothing about ability; it is only the birth-date window a child is registered in.

Why did the cutoff change?

From 2017 to 2026 age groups followed the calendar year, which split school classes: a child born in September played a year above most of their classmates. From the 2026-27 season the three national youth organizations went back to an August 1 to July 31 window, so age groups line up with school years again.

My child has a fall birthday. Why didn’t they move up this year?

Because the window moved with them. A child born in October 2017 was U-9 in 2025-26 under the calendar rule and is U-9 again in 2026-27 under the August rule. They have not lost a year; the groups shifted around them. Most of their classmates are now in the same group, which is the point of the change.

Can my child play up?

Usually yes, one age group, with the club’s approval. Playing down is not allowed in registered play except for documented developmental reasons. Ask your club before you assume either.

Does every league use this?

US Club Soccer, US Youth Soccer (including the state associations such as ENYYSA) and AYSO all adopted the August window for 2026-27. High school soccer follows school-grade eligibility, not these groups. If your league or town program has its own rule, its registration page will say so; when in doubt, ask the registrar.

What proof of age do we need?

A birth certificate or passport at first registration. Clubs keep it on file, and the player card carries the birth year, so opponents and referees can check it at games.
