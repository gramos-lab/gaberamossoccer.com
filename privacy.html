Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Gabe Ramos Soccer (“we,” “us”) is a business name of Four Point Ventures LLC, based on Long Island, New York. This policy explains what information we collect through gaberamossoccer.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions can be sent to gramos@royalsportinggroup.com.

What we collect

Information you send us. When you use the contact form we receive your name, email address, club or organization (if you provide it), the inquiry type you select, and your message.

Booking information. When you book a training session or course through this site we collect the parent or guardian’s name, email, and phone number; the player’s name and age; the club and position you tell us; any notes about goals or health considerations you choose to share; and the date, time, and location of the session.

Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card number is entered on Stripe’s secure checkout and is never seen or stored by us. We receive confirmation of payment, the amount, and the last four digits of the card for receipts and refunds.

Waiver records. When a parent or guardian accepts our participant waiver, we record the version accepted, the date and time, and the network address it was accepted from.

Site analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which reports aggregate page views and performance without cookies, fingerprinting, or tracking individual visitors.

We do not use advertising cookies or sell any information.

How we use it

We use the information above to respond to inquiries, schedule and deliver sessions and courses, send booking confirmations, reminders, and receipts, keep required waiver and safety records, process refunds, and improve the site. If you have opted in to course announcements or updates, we will email you about upcoming courses and programs; every such email includes an unsubscribe link.

Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers who handle data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes:

  • Cloudflare — website hosting, security, and cookie-free analytics
  • Web3Forms — delivery of contact form submissions to our email
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Supabase — secure storage of booking records
  • Resend — transactional email (confirmations, reminders, receipts)

We may also share information when required by law, or to protect the safety of players, coaches, or the public.

Children

Our training and courses involve players under 18. All player information is provided by a parent or legal guardian, who books and manages the account. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us directly, email gramos@royalsportinggroup.com and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

Contact inquiries are kept for up to two years. Booking, payment, and waiver records are kept for as long as needed for safety, accounting, and legal purposes, generally seven years. You can ask us to delete your information sooner where we are not required to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by emailing gramos@royalsportinggroup.com. You can unsubscribe from any announcement email using the link in the message. If you contact us from California or another state with privacy rights, we will honor those rights as they apply.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with providers that maintain industry-standard safeguards. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify you as required by law if a breach affects your information.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date at the top. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
